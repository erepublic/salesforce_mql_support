# MQLs vs Opportunities for Sales

## Short Version

An **MQL** is a **qualified person who is ready for sales attention**.

An **Opportunity** is the **deal or sales pursuit** you are actively working.

If you remember one thing, remember this:

- **MQL = the signal**
- **Opportunity = the deal**

## What An MQL Means

In this system, an MQL is created when a contact shows enough buying intent or engagement to merit sales follow-up. That can come from:

- marketing behavior crossing a threshold
- a hand-raiser action such as a form or portal request
- other intake paths that now route through the MQL process

For Sales, an MQL answers:

- **Who is showing intent?**
- **Why are they surfacing now?**
- **What product or source triggered the follow-up?**

An MQL is tied to a **Contact**, not to pipeline forecasting. It is primarily a qualification and routing record.

## What An Opportunity Means

An Opportunity is the record for the actual revenue conversation or deal motion.

For Sales, an Opportunity answers:

- **What deal are we working?**
- **What stage is it in?**
- **Who owns it?**
- **What is the expected revenue / close timing?**

This is the object that represents pipeline progression, active sales work, and forecasting.

## Why The System Uses Both

The system separates **lead qualification** from **deal management**.

- Use the **MQL** to understand the reason a person was sent to Sales.
- Use the **Opportunity** to manage the actual selling process.

That separation matters because the same person can show new marketing intent even when a deal already exists.

## Practical Rule

- If Sales needs to know **why this person surfaced**, look at the **MQL**.
- If Sales needs to know **what deal is being worked**, look at the **Opportunity**.

## What Usually Happens In Salesforce

- If a contact is qualified and has **no open Opportunity**, the system can create **both an MQL and an Opportunity**.
- If a contact is qualified but is **already tied to an open Opportunity**, the system may create **the MQL only** and link Sales back to the existing deal.
- Some direct-sales or operational paths can still go **straight to an Opportunity** without a separate MQL.

## Article-Ready Blurb

An MQL is a marketing-qualified person: someone whose behavior or hand-raiser activity shows they are ready for sales attention. An Opportunity is the deal record Sales works once there is an active revenue conversation. In short, the MQL explains **why this person matters now**, while the Opportunity tracks **the actual deal and pipeline stage**.
